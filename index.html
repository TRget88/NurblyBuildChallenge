Nurbly Build Challenge Register

Design a drone in six months. In public.

The Nurbly Build Challenge is a six-month virtual CAD competition. Each month opens a new stage. By the end, your team has designed one complete drone. Every file lives in a public Nurbly repo, so the whole design history is on the record. Teams can be any size. Teams of three or fewer use Nurbly free. Teams of four or more need a paid Nurbly plan for the run.

Key dates

  • Registration opens 1 Dec 2026
  • Stage 1 opens 1 Jan 2027
  • Stage deadlines Last Sunday of each month, 23:59 UTC
  • Final deadline 27 Jun 2027, 23:59 UTC

Stages

StageMonthDeliverable
1. AirframeJanArms, body, landing gear and motor mounts as STEP. Printable or machinable.
2. Electronics layoutFebFlight controller, ESC and battery mounts, cable routing. PCB outline if custom.
3. PropulsionMarMotor selection and prop guards. Thrust math documented in the repo.
4. Control and sensingAprFC mount, camera or sensor gimbal, antenna placement.
5. IntegrationMayFull assembly, interference check, mass budget, BOM.
6. FinalJunDocumented build, optional physical build photos, release tag.

How it is judged

  • Engineering quality 35%
  • Design process 25%
  • Integration with prior stages 20%
  • Documentation 15%
  • Presentation 5%

Commit history is judged, so a single deadline-day commit scores below a month of small commits.

Rules summary

  • One public Nurbly repo per team, tagged nbc-2027.
  • All models as STEP.
  • Tag each stage stage-N.
  • At least 10 commits per stage.
  • Any CAD tool.
  • Late joiners welcome from any stage.
  • Participants keep their IP.
  • You must be 18 or older.

FAQ

Do I need to build the drone physically?

No. A physical build is optional. It only earns a bonus in stage 6.

What CAD tool do I need?

Any tool that exports STEP.

What does Nurbly cost?

Nothing for teams of three or fewer. Teams of four or more need a paid plan for the run.

Can I join late?

Yes. You can join from any stage.

Where do I ask questions?

On our Discord.
